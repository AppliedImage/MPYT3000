Key Mappings (in Seconds)

Press keys 0–9 to jump to the corresponding time in the selected video. The "Auto-Pause" checkbox defines the default behavior (if checked, the video will auto-pause after the specified duration). Hold Shift while pressing the key to invert the default. In all cases, the video is unpaused immediately.
